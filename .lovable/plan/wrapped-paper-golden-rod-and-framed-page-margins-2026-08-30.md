# Wrapped-Paper Golden Rod and Framed Page Margins

## Goal
Make the opened invitation read as one physical scroll: the same ornate golden rod remains, but a matching ivory handmade-paper layer visibly wraps around its cylinder, and all page content sits comfortably within the resulting scroll frame.

## Phase 1 — Create and review the rod PNG
1. Edit the existing golden-rod artwork rather than generating a different rod.
2. Preserve its carved end caps, gold finish, proportions, transparency, and full visual thickness.
3. Add a realistic strip of the invitation’s warm ivory handmade paper wrapping around the central gold cylinder, with subtle curvature, shading, and matching paper texture.
4. Keep the outer area transparent so the asset layers cleanly over the site.
5. Produce one high-resolution composite PNG; the bottom instance can be rotated so both paper lips face inward toward the page.
6. Present the PNG for visual review and stop. Do not replace the live rods until approval is given.

## Phase 2 — Install only after asset approval
1. Upload the approved PNG through the project asset flow and replace both current rod instances with it.
2. Preserve the existing center-to-top/bottom unfurl animation and equal rod sizing.
3. Layer the continuous page beneath the paper-wrapped section of each rod, while clipping scrolling artwork and text inside the visible frame.
4. Add responsive inset margins on all four sides:
   - left/right margins align the paper and content with the usable width between the carved end caps;
   - top/bottom safe areas align content below/above the inward-facing wrapped paper portions;
   - mobile spacing remains compact without horizontal overflow.
5. Keep the header strip above the top rod unchanged.

## Verification
- Review the new standalone PNG first at full size and against transparency.
- After approval and installation, verify closed, opening, and open states on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm both rods are undistorted and equal in size, paper appears physically wrapped into them, content never crosses the rod boundaries, and the page has balanced margins on every side.
